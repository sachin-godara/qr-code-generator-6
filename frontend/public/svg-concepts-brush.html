Pure SVG Vector Recreations

These are 100% code-based (HTML/SVG) recreations of the AI brush-stroke logo. They are infinitely scalable, lightweight, and perfect for favicons or web logos.

Variant 1: Clean Geometric

Using perfectly round-capped lines for a modern, tech-focused brush look.

Variant 2: Tapered Brush

Using custom SVG paths to simulate the tapering effect of a real paint brush.

Variant 3: Blend & Splatter

Simulating the chaotic ink splatters and overlapping colors from the AI version.

Variant 4: True Transparency

The tapered design shown over a checkerboard to prove the rounded corners and edges are fully transparent.

Variant 5: Minimalist (Favicon-512)

The clean, high-contrast, geometric version (no brush strokes) that scales perfectly for tiny favicons.
