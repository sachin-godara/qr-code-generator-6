Want people to find your location easily? A Google Maps QR code lets anyone scan and instantly get turn-by-turn directions — no typing addresses, no searching, no spelling mistakes.

This guide shows you how to create one in under 2 minutes.

## Step 1: Get Your Google Maps Link

### Option A: From Google Maps Website

1. Go to [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com).
2. **Search for your location** or drop a pin at the exact spot.
3. Click the **Share** button (or the location name/address).
4. Click **Copy link** — you'll get a URL like:
   `https://maps.google.com/maps?q=40.7580,-73.9855`

### Option B: From Google Maps App (Mobile)

1. Open the Google Maps app on your phone.
2. **Find your location** and tap on the pin.
3. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom.
4. Tap **Copy to clipboard**.

### Option C: Using Coordinates

If you have exact GPS coordinates, create the URL manually:
```
https://www.google.com/maps?q=LATITUDE,LONGITUDE
```
For example: `https://www.google.com/maps?q=48.8584,2.2945` (Eiffel Tower)

## Best Use Cases for Location QR Codes

### Wedding Invitations

Print a QR code on your invitation that opens the venue location in Google Maps. Guests won't need to search for the address — just scan and navigate.

**Tip:** Use a decorative dot style (like "heart" or "flower") and match the QR code colors to your wedding theme.

### Business Storefronts

Place a QR code in your window or on your business card that links to your Google Maps listing. This helps customers:
- Get driving directions
- See your business hours
- Read reviews
- Call your business directly

### Event Venues

For conferences, concerts, or meetups, include a location QR code on:
- Ticket confirmation emails
- Social media event posts
- Printed programs and signage

### Delivery Instructions

Share your exact location with delivery drivers by texting or emailing a QR code. This is especially useful for:
- Locations without clear addresses
- Rural or new developments
- Large complexes with multiple entrances

### Tourism & Travel

Hotels, tour companies, and travel guides can use QR codes to:
- Link to points of interest
- Provide walking tour routes
- Mark pickup/dropoff locations

## Pro Tips for Location QR Codes

### Use Google Maps Plus Codes

For locations without a street address, use a [Plus Code](https://plus.codes/):
```
https://www.google.com/maps?q=plus+code+here
```

### Add Custom Labels

You can include a label in the Google Maps URL:
```
https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Central+Park+New+York
```
This displays the location name when the map opens.

### Use Apple Maps as Fallback

If your audience is primarily iPhone users, consider creating a universal link that works with both:
```
https://maps.apple.com/?q=40.7580,-73.9855
```
Or use a service like `maps.google.com` which works on all platforms — Google Maps will open in the browser even for iPhone users who don't have the app installed.

### Add a CTA Frame

Don't just print a bare QR code. Add a text frame with a clear call-to-action:
