## Top 7 QR Code Trends in 2025

### 1. GS1 Digital Link — QR Codes Replace Barcodes at Checkout

The biggest structural change in QR code history is underway. **GS1**, the organization behind UPC barcodes, has launched the **Digital Link** standard that encodes product URLs into QR codes.

- **Walmart**, **Carrefour**, and **Kroger** are piloting QR-at-checkout systems
- Target rollout: **2027** for major retailers globally
- Impact: Every product on every shelf will eventually have a QR code instead of (or alongside) a barcode

This means QR codes are transitioning from marketing tools to **core retail infrastructure**.

### 2. Designed QR Codes Are the New Standard

Plain black-and-white QR codes are fading. In 2025, branded and designed QR codes are expected by consumers:

- **Custom dot styles**: Rounded, diamond, heart, fluid shapes
- **Brand colors and gradients**: Matching corporate palettes
- **Logo embedding**: Company logos in the center
- **CTA text frames**: "Scan to Order," "Download App," etc.

### 4. AR-Powered QR Experiences

Augmented reality QR codes are emerging. Instead of linking to a webpage, scanning a QR code launches an AR experience:

- **Product packaging**: Scan to see 3D product models
- **Real estate**: Scan a yard sign to see the property in AR
- **Education**: Scan textbook images for interactive 3D anatomy, chemistry models
- **Retail**: "Try on" products virtually through QR-triggered AR

Apple's Vision Pro and Google's AR integration in Search are accelerating this trend.

### 5. Sustainability-Driven QR Code Adoption

QR codes are replacing printed materials across industries:

- **Restaurant menus**: 72% of US restaurants used QR menus in 2024 (up from 8% pre-COVID)
- **Product manuals**: QR codes on packaging link to digital manuals (saving paper)
- **Business cards**: Digital business cards via [vCard QR codes](/vcard-qr-code/) reduce card waste
- **Event tickets**: Digital QR tickets eliminate printed ticket stock

### 6. Dynamic QR Codes for Enterprise

While [GenerateCustomQR](/) specializes in **static** QR codes (data embedded directly — no tracking, no expiry), enterprise platforms are expanding dynamic QR code features:

- **Real-time URL changing** without reprinting
- **Scan analytics** (location, device, time)
- **A/B testing** different landing pages from the same QR code
- **Expiry controls** for time-limited promotions

For most individual and small business use cases, static QR codes remain the best choice — they're free, permanent, private, and don't require subscriptions.

### 7. Security and Authentication QR Codes

QR codes are increasingly used for security purposes:

- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)**: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator
- **Digital identity**: India's Aadhaar, EU Digital Identity
- **COVID health passes**: Established the precedent for QR-based credentials
- **Secure document signing**: QR codes on legal documents for verification
