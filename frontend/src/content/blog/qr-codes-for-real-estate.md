# QR Codes for Real Estate — A Complete Guide for Agents & Brokers

Real estate is one of the fastest-growing sectors for QR code adoption. From yard signs to open house flyers, QR codes bridge the gap between physical marketing and digital property information.

## Why Real Estate Agents Need QR Codes

Traditional real estate marketing has a major limitation: physical materials can only hold so much information. A yard sign shows an address and phone number — but buyers want photos, virtual tours, floor plans, pricing history, and neighborhood data.

QR codes solve this by turning any physical surface into a portal to rich digital content:

- **Yard signs** → Link to the full MLS listing with 50+ photos
- **Business cards** → Save agent contact info instantly via vCard
- **Open house flyers** → Direct to a 3D virtual tour
- **Window displays** → Show available properties 24/7, even after hours
- **Print ads** → Track engagement and measure ROI

## 7 QR Code Use Cases for Real Estate

### 1. Property Listing QR Codes

Create a QR code that links to the full MLS listing or property page. Place it on:
- Yard signs and directional signs
- Flyer boxes and brochure holders
- Window displays at the property
- Print advertisements in magazines

**Best practice:** Use a URL QR code pointing to a responsive property page with photos, pricing, and a contact form. [Create a URL QR code →](/url-qr-code-generator)

### 2. Virtual Tour QR Codes

Link directly to a Matterport, Zillow 3D Home, or YouTube virtual tour. Buyers can explore the property on their phone right from the curb.

**Best practice:** Place the QR code at eye level on the front door or window with a label like *"Scan for Virtual Tour"*.

### 3. Agent Contact vCard QR Codes

Create a QR code that saves your full contact information — name, phone, email, company, and photo — to the buyer's phone with a single scan.

**Best practice:** Add your vCard QR code to business cards, email signatures, and name badges. [Create a vCard QR code →](/vcard-qr-code)

### 4. Open House Registration

Use a QR code that links to a sign-in form (Google Forms, Typeform, or your CRM). Visitors scan at the door instead of signing a paper sheet — you get clean digital leads.

### 5. Neighborhood Information

Create QR codes for neighborhood guides — school ratings, walkability scores, nearby amenities, commute times. Buyers making location decisions appreciate this context.

### 6. Mortgage Calculator

Link to an online mortgage calculator pre-filled with the property price. This helps buyers quickly estimate monthly payments.

### 7. Document Downloads

Use QR codes on listing presentations to link to downloadable PDFs: inspection reports, HOA documents, floor plans, and seller disclosures.

## Designing Real Estate QR Codes

### Color and Branding

Match your QR code colors to your brokerage brand:
- **RE/MAX**: Red and blue accent
- **Keller Williams**: Red and gray
- **Coldwell Banker**: Navy blue
- **Independent agents**: Use your personal brand colors

### Size for Yard Signs

Yard signs are typically scanned from **3–10 meters away** (car window or sidewalk). Follow these minimums:

| Placement | Minimum QR Size | Recommended Size |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Rider sign (top panel) | 8 cm × 8 cm | 10–12 cm × 10–12 cm |
| Main sign panel | 10 cm × 10 cm | 15 cm × 15 cm |
| Window display | 12 cm × 12 cm | 18 cm × 18 cm |
| Directional sign | 10 cm × 10 cm | 12 cm × 12 cm |

**Pro tip:** Export as SVG for crisp edges at any print size. [Learn more about sizing →](/blog/qr-code-size-guide)

### Adding a Call-to-Action

Never place a bare QR code without context. Always include a clear label:

- ✅ *"Scan for Photos & Pricing"*
- ✅ *"View Virtual Tour"*
- ✅ *"Save My Contact Info"*
- ❌ *"Scan Me"* (too vague — buyers won't bother)
