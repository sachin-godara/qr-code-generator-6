## Step 1: Prepare Your Menu Online

Before you create a QR code, your menu needs to live somewhere online. Here are the most common options:

| Platform | Best For | Cost |
|----------|----------|------|
| **Google Sites** | Simple, free, fast setup | Free |
| **Your own website** | Full branding control | Varies |
| **PDF hosted on Google Drive** | Quick and easy | Free |
| **Dedicated menu platforms** (e.g., MenuTiger) | Advanced features | Freemium |

> **Pro Tip:** If you host a PDF on Google Drive, make sure the sharing link is set to "Anyone with the link can view." Test the link in an incognito browser window before generating your QR code.

### Design Tips for Restaurant QR Codes

- **Use your brand colors** — Match the QR code palette to your restaurant's identity
- **Add your logo** — A centered logo builds instant recognition and trust
- **Choose rounded dot styles** — They feel more inviting than sharp squares
- **Add a text frame** — Use a CTA like "Scan for Menu" or "View Our Specials"
- **Keep contrast high** — Dark dots on light background ensures scannability in any lighting

## Step 3: Print and Place Your QR Codes

The placement of your QR code matters just as much as the design:

### Best Placement Options

- **Table tents** — Stand-up cards on each table (most popular)
- **Stickers on tables** — Weatherproof vinyl stickers embedded in the table surface
- **Counter displays** — Acrylic stands at the register or bar
- **Window decals** — Visible from outside to attract walk-ins
- **Receipt prints** — Add the QR code to receipts for repeat visits

### Print Specifications

- **Minimum size:** 2 × 2 cm (0.8 × 0.8 inches) for close-range scanning
- **Recommended size:** 4 × 4 cm or larger for table tents
- **Format:** Use **SVG** or **PDF** from GenerateCustomQR for lossless scaling
- **Resolution:** At least 300 DPI for professional print quality

## Step 4: Test Before You Deploy

Before printing hundreds of copies:

1. **Test with multiple phones** — Try iOS Camera, Google Lens, and at least one third-party scanner app
2. **Test in restaurant lighting** — Scan in actual ambient conditions (dim lighting can affect dark-on-dark designs)
3. **Test the landing page** — Make sure your menu loads fast on mobile and is readable without zooming
